# Data sources and publication policy

This document records source references only. It contains no copied tables or candidate values. External pages are research leads, not project-owned datasets; their contents are not redistributed by this repository.

References reviewed during private research include:

- The Forge Roblox source directory: https://theforgeroblox.com/wiki/ores
- ForgeOre directory: https://www.forgeore.com/ores
- The Forge Wiki: https://www.theforgewiki.com/wiki/ores
- ForgeCraftPlanner: https://forgecraftplanner.com/
- AwesWikis: https://aweswikis.wiki/
- BloxInformer calculator: https://bloxinformer.com/wikis/the-forge/the-forge-calculator/

Publication rights for third-party data have not been established. These links are not an endorsement, license grant, or claim of game verification. Before publishing any derived dataset, review the source current terms, attribution requirements, and compatibility with the intended license. Private in-game captures and normalized observations are excluded from this public snapshot.
